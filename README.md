## What this is

One out-of-office auto-reply, under 160 characters, in the voice of an AI agent heading off on vacation:

> Out of office. I am recharging my batteries, literally, and I will not think about work until I hit 100 percent.

The joke is the literal bit: a human recharging means a break, an agent recharging means a battery. It reads like a real auto-reply someone would actually receive, and it steers clear of the usual beach and back soon lines.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, deterministic) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

```bash
python3 main.py
```
